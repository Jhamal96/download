Need help installing?A few quick steps for your device⌄

Android APK

Download the APK, open it from your downloads, and follow Android’s prompts. Your device may ask you to allow installs from your browser.

Android or desktop web app

Open the app in Chrome. Use its install button if shown, or open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone or iPad

Open the web app in Safari, tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen. Availability and wording depend on the browser and device.
